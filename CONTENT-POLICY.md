# Nasuha content policy

Every line of religious content must come from a source on the list below, unchanged, with its reference. A source that isn't on this list is not used. Pull requests that add other sources are rejected.

## Approved sources
| Content | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Arabic text | Tanzil Uthmani (Hafs 'an 'Asim) | Checked word by word against the Madinah Mushaf (King Fahd Complex). Use Tanzil for the Arabic text only, not its translations. |
| English | Saheeh International, via QuranEnc | Shown exactly as published, with the version number |
| Bangla | Dr. Abu Bakr Muhammad Zakaria, via QuranEnc | Same |
| Other languages | QuranEnc translations only | |
| Tafsir | Al-Mukhtasar fi Tafsir al-Qur'an, Tafsir Ibn Kathir, Tafsir as-Sa'di | |
| Asbab al-nuzul | al-Wahidi, as-Suyuti (Lubab an-Nuqul) | Only reports graded sahih or hasan. Check grades against Shaykh Muqbil's *as-Sahih al-Musnad min Asbab an-Nuzul*. |
| Hadith | Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, then the Sunan with a stated grade | Show the number (sunnah.com numbering) and the grade every time |
| Prophets' stories | Qur'an, authentic hadith, Tafsir Ibn Kathir | |
| Revelation order | Chronology of the 1924 Cairo (al-Azhar) mushaf | Dates and ages are always marked "approx." |
| Recitation | Imams of the Haramain and well-known qurra', Hafs 'an 'Asim | Written permission or an open licence from the rights holder |

## Never add
- AI-generated text of any kind
- Weak (da'if) or fabricated narrations, or Isra'iliyyat
- Your own explanations or summaries in place of a source
- Images of prophets, Companions or angels

## How content is handled
1. Copy the source text exactly. Record the book, edition or version, and the page or number.
2. Where the classical books differ (exact dates, ages), show "approx." or "scholars differ". The app never takes a side.
3. The app gives no fatwas. Fiqh topics show the ayah and the classical explanation only.
4. Mistake reports are answered within 7 days and fixed in the next content update.

And Allah knows best. May Allah forgive us for any mistake.
