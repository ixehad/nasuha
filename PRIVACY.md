# Nasuha: privacy and terms

Last updated 12 November 2026. This page mirrors the "Privacy and terms" screen in the app.

## Privacy

In short: nothing about you ever leaves your phone.

**No account, no tracking.** You never sign up. We do not collect your name, email, location or how you use the app. There are no ads and no analytics.

**What stays on your phone.** Your name (if you add one), topics, reminder time, languages, saved ayahs and reading progress. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

**Reminders.** Reminders are set up on your iPhone itself. No server knows when or what you read.

**Languages.** To decide which languages to offer, the app reads the region and language set on your iPhone. It does not use your location, and this never leaves your phone.

**Recitation.** The Qur'an text and translations are inside the app. When you play a recitation, the audio file downloads from the recitation host. That host sees a normal web request, like any website. We send nothing about you.

**Tips.** Tips are paid through Apple. We never see your card or Apple ID details.

**Report a mistake.** Reporting a mistake opens your own email app or our public issue page. You choose what to send.

**Children.** Nasuha is safe for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

**Changes.** If this ever changes, we will tell you in the app before it happens.

## Terms

In short: free to use, sources always named, and not a replacement for a scholar.

**Free to use.** Nasuha is free. Tips are optional and give no extra features.

**Not a fatwa.** Stories and explanations are taken from the sources we name. They are for learning and reminding, not religious rulings. For a ruling, ask a qualified scholar.

**Mistakes.** We check everything with care, but we are human. If you find a mistake, tap Report a mistake and we will fix it, in sha Allah.

**Respect for the Qur'an.** Please share ayahs respectfully and with their reference.

**Content licences.** The Arabic text, translations, tafsir and recitation belong to their owners and are used under their licences, listed in About us and in [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md).

**The code.** The app's code is public under the PolyForm Noncommercial licence. You may read, copy and change it for non-commercial use. You may not sell it, and the Nasuha name and logo are not included.

**No warranty.** The app is provided as it is. We are not responsible for missed reminders caused by your phone settings.
